# Progress: G1 preregistration amendment v5

STATUS: revised RFC; v4 superseded before activation.

WHAT: separates statistical efficacy (`LB90 > 0`) from deployment eligibility
(`LB90 > MEE`), sets a distinct planning alternative for power, and replaces
the inaccurate "observation-free" wording with a blinded, excluded calibration
pilot. The amended protocol fixes sample-size selection, block selection,
no-peeking, and terminal-record requirements.

WHY/DIR: v4 would have let any statistically positive but economically trivial
effect trigger GO, while treating 40 real sessions as if they were not data.
That is not an acceptable deployment experiment.

EVIDENCE: the earlier v3/v4 simulation exposed the original power contradiction;
this amendment preserves that finding but uses a deployment threshold and a
separate planning alternative correctly.

NEXT: independent review; implement the blinded pilot and revised simulation;
do not activate, measure a terminal endpoint, or change capital deployment
until all acceptance conditions in the v5 amendment are met.
